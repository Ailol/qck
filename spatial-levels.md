Author: Ailo. Recorded 7 November 2026.

This page preserves the author's vocabulary for spatial and level organisation in qck. The labels below describe this project's conceptual model; the browser implementation uses them as view and interaction levels.

## Original definition

The following two blocks preserve the wording supplied by Ailo:

```text
earth'sets of reality{
1d(fabric is one 1d, all internet is that) 
2d is spatial,
3d is when time vector and everything comes in to play
}

e'tensick{
4d is your mind within the proximity of self change.
}
```

The distinction matters: the first block names fabric, spatial placement and the time vector. The second names a personal layer of self-change. In Ailo's description, after 3d the journey is personal and develops through training. These labels do not imply an automatic progression through an unlimited numbered ladder.

The related human formulation in the author's supplied notes is:

```text
human'sets of reality{
1d(fabric is one 1d, all internet is that)
2d is spatial,
3d is when time vector and everything comes in to play
}
e'tenzor[happenz when you make own choicez]{
```

`e'tensick` and `e'tenzor` are preserved as written in the source formulations. This page does not silently merge or redefine them.

## Interface mapping

| Level | Project term | Browser behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| 1d | fabric | Show the connection fabric and its anchors. |
| 2d | spatial | Show node boundaries and positions; move nodes while retaining links. |
| 3d | time vector | Scrub or play a time parameter along those links. |
| 4d | current / self-change | Add a user-selected anchor and user-entered current context to that view. |

The browser's 4d view is an editable representation of context, not a measurement of someone's mind. Switching a view does not change a person's mental state. The model's use of dimensional labels is distinct from conventional mathematical or physical dimensions.

## Keeping the explanation available

The source wording is versioned here so it can be read and discussed without relying on a disappearing preview or a chat recollection. Ailo described regretting not writing it down earlier and wanting to preserve the explanation.

Colour and background are presentation settings. They can express a chosen view without changing the definitions above. The interface exports those settings as JSON; this page remains the textual reference for the level vocabulary.
